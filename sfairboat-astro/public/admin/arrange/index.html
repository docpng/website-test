Arrange your pages

Drag photos and videos from your computer onto any page, move them around, and reorder sections. Sign in with the GitHub account you use for the site editor.

Sign in with an access token instead

Paste a GitHub personal access token with write access to the site's repository.

How it works

  • Add a photo or video: drag it from your computer onto the page. Blue lines show where it can go, between sections or between paragraphs of text.
  • Move things: hover a section and drag ⠿ Move, or drag a photo/video to a new spot.
  • Change a photo or video: click it to set its size, position, caption and description.
  • Publish saves everything. The live site updates in about 1–2 minutes.

Text, colors and other settings are edited in the site editor.
